Decoder-Only Transformer Language Model from Scratch

NC State · Software–Hardware Co-design
PythonPyTorchCUDA

Problem

Understand the transformer end to end by implementing every component, then measure how architecture choices change quality and cost.

What I did

  • Implemented causal multi-head attention, positional encoding, a byte-level BPE tokenizer, and the training and generation pipeline in PyTorch.
  • Trained on TinyStories on an NVIDIA RTX 2080 Ti (batch 16, sequence length 512, 3 epochs).
  • Ran controlled experiments on model size (Tiny ~5M vs Small ~13M parameters) and normalization position (pre-norm vs post-norm).

Result

Scaling to ~13M parameters cut validation perplexity from 38.2 to 22.3; post-norm reduced it to 34.2 vs. pre-norm.
