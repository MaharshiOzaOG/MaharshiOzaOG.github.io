FIR Filter Accelerator via High-Level Synthesis

NC State · Hardware–Software Codesign
SystemCCatapult HLSRISC-V

Problem

Offload FIR filtering from a RISC-V processor to a dedicated accelerator.

What I did

  • Implemented the accelerator in SystemC as an FSM with 64-bit packed data channels for coefficients, inputs and outputs.
  • Synthesized it with Catapult HLS at a 5 ns clock and compared architectures by area, latency and critical path.

Result

Latency reduced from ~3.5K cycles to 69 cycles at a ~2.8 ns critical path.
