Pipelined Transformer Self-Attention in Hardware

NC State · ASIC Design
Verilog HDL

Problem

Compute Transformer self-attention efficiently in hardware.

What I did

  • Designed a deeply pipelined self-attention datapath with SRAM-based matrix storage.
  • Optimized the critical path and validated timing, area, latency and synthesis correctness.

Result

Clock period reduced by 50%, reaching an area–performance efficiency of 3.97 × 10⁻⁹ ns⁻¹·µm⁻².
