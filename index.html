About

I build and verify hardware. At Maxgen Technologies I wrote an out-of-order AXI4 scoreboard in SystemVerilog/UVM and contributed to packet-router RTL; in my M.S. at NC State I've designed and verified caches, accelerators and processor components, taken designs through synthesis, place-and-route, low-power and DFT flows, and reverse-engineered the microarchitecture of real x86 processors.

I'm especially interested in where hardware meets AI: profiling LLM inference with hardware performance counters, quantization and pruning for efficient inference, building a transformer from scratch, and HLS accelerators. Before grad school I spent two years at Magna Electronics on ADAS camera perception. I'm looking for roles in design verification, RTL and CPU design, or hardware for AI in the US.
